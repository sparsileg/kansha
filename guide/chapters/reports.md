# Reports

::: {.note title="At a glance"}
Pick a report from the **Reports** menu. It opens with sensible
settings; change the date range and the choices on its bar, or
**Customize…** it further. Click any amount to see the transactions
behind it. Save a report you'll run again, and export it to CSV or
PDF.
:::

## The Reports Menu

| Menu | Reports |
|:---|:---|
| Investing | Capital Gains, Investment Performance, Investment Income, Holdings, Asset Allocation |
| Net Worth | Net Worth |
| Spending | Itemized Categories, Itemized Payees, Income/Expense by Category, Income/Expense by Payee |
| Comparison | Current Spending vs. Average by Category, by Payee |
| Tax | Capital Gains, Tax Schedule, Tax Summary |
| Saved Reports | The reports you've saved, in their folders |

[Reports in Detail](appendix-reports.md) describes each one.

## The Report Window

::: {.shot id="report-window"}
Income/Expense by Category for last year: the bar with Date range
and Interval, the buttons Customize…, Save…, Expand all, Collapse
all, Export CSV, and Save PDF…, and the report with INCOME and
EXPENSES sections and a total.
:::

The bar across the top holds the choices you change most:

- **Date range**: a preset such as **Year to date**, **Last month**,
  **Last 12 months**, or **Custom dates…**. Some presets add a second
  list to pick which month, quarter, or year.
- **Interval** (the two Income/Expense reports): one column per
  week, month, quarter, year, and so on, or **None** for one column.
  Net Worth has its Interval under **Customize… > Display**.
- **Subtotal by** (Capital Gains, Tax Summary, the comparisons): how
  the rows are grouped. The itemized reports have theirs under
  **Customize… > Display**: by category (or payee), or by tag first.
- **Sort by** (the itemized and tax reports): the order of the rows.
- **Compare to** (the comparisons): the past periods to average.

The buttons:

| Button | What it does |
|:---|:---|
| Customize… | Opens the full settings; see below |
| Save… / Save / Save as… | Keeps the report with its settings; see Saved Reports |
| Expand all, Collapse all | Opens or folds every group |
| Export CSV | Saves the report as a CSV file in Downloads, for a spreadsheet |
| Save PDF… | Saves it as a PDF, portrait or landscape, and opens it in your PDF viewer to print |

Reports with a graph have **Hide graph** and **Hide report** above
each part.

### See the transactions behind a number

Click any amount. Kansha opens the transactions that make it up,
each of which opens in its register. A group's heading folds its rows
away and brings them back.

::: tip
Before you trust a category's total, click it. A transaction in the
wrong category is easy to spot in the list, and fixing it fixes the
report.
:::

## Customize

**Customize…** opens the full settings, in tabs.

**Display:**

- **Title** of the report.
- **Totals only**: the totals without the rows under them.
- **Totals on group heading**: a group's total on its heading line,
  as the tax forms show it.
- **Cents (no rounding)**: show cents, or round to whole dollars.
- **Accounts with zero balances** (Net Worth).
- **Transfers** and **Cash flow (transfers to other accounts)**
  (Income/Expense): whether money moved between accounts shows.
  With Cash flow, money moved to or from accounts not chosen on the
  Accounts tab is listed in a TRANSFERS section.
- **Show columns**: which columns appear; **Reset columns** puts them
  back.

**Accounts, Categories, Payees, Securities, Tags:** which ones the
report includes. **Select all** and **Clear all** do what they say;
**Contains** narrows the list as you type; **Show hidden and closed**
lists those too.

::: tip
To see one account's spending, open Income/Expense by Category, go
to **Customize… > Accounts**, click **Clear all**, and tick that one
account.
:::

## Saved Reports

**Save…** keeps a report with all its settings, date range included,
under a name you give it, in a folder. **Save as…** keeps a copy
under a new name. The saved report appears under **Reports > Saved
Reports**, in its folder.

**Reports > Saved Reports > Manage Saved Reports…** opens, renames,
moves, and deletes saved reports, and makes folders. **Unfiled**
holds those not in a folder; it can't be deleted.

::: tip
Save a report with a relative date range, such as **Last year** or
**Last month**, and it shows the right dates every time you open it.
:::

## Tax Reports

The three Tax reports gather what your return needs:

- **Tax Schedule** lists each tax line, such as Schedule A charitable
  gifts or Schedule B interest, with the transactions under it. It
  follows the tax lines set on your categories and accounts (see
  [Categories, Payees, and Tags](categories.md)). Non-cash gifts to
  charity over $500 are marked **(Form 8283 needed)**.
- **Tax Summary** lists every tax-related transaction, subtotaled by
  category, tax line, account, payee, tag, or period.
- **Capital Gains** lists every sale with its proceeds, cost basis,
  and gain, short-term and long-term apart. It leaves out the
  tax-deferred and tax-exempt accounts.

All three start at **Last year**.

::: why
Kansha's numbers come from what you entered. Your broker's 1099-B and
the bank's 1099-INT are the numbers the IRS has. Compare them before
you file; if they differ, find out why, and fix Kansha or ask the
broker.
:::

## Printing

**Save PDF…** asks for portrait or landscape, saves the PDF, and
opens it in your PDF viewer. Print from there.
