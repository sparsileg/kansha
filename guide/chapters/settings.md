# Settings and Themes

::: {.note title="At a glance"}
**Edit > Settings…** holds the book's settings, in six groups. They're
kept in the book, so they move with it. The theme, font, and text
size are on the menu bar and belong to this computer. **Edit >
Navigation Bar…** chooses the buttons along the top.
:::

## Settings

Choose **Edit > Settings…**. Pick a group on the left; its settings
show on the right.

::: {.shot id="settings"}
The Settings dialog on the Interface group: Date format, First day of
week, On startup open to, Account list side, Show cents in account
bar balances, and Rows shown on spending cards.
:::

### Interface

| Setting | What it does |
|:---|:---|
| Date format | How dates are shown and typed |
| First day of week | Sunday or Monday, for the Calendar and weekly reports |
| On startup open to | The screen Kansha shows when the book opens |
| Account list side | The Accounts panel on the left or the right |
| Show cents in account bar balances | Cents in the Accounts panel, or whole dollars |
| Rows shown on spending cards (more rows scroll) | See [Insights](insights.md) |

### Data

| Setting | What it does |
|:---|:---|
| Integrity check at startup | Checks the book each time it opens |
| Due soon shows items due within (days) | The Due soon card's reach |

### Investments

| Setting | What it does |
|:---|:---|
| Price is stale after (days) | When Needs attention flags an old price |
| Lot method for new investment accounts | Their default lot method |
| Allow price download (internet) | Lets **Download prices** go online |

### Register

| Setting | What it does |
|:---|:---|
| Gray reconciled transactions | Shows reconciled rows in gray |
| Recall memorized payees | Fills in from a payee's last use |
| Automatically memorize new payees | Remembers new payees as you enter them |
| Capitalize payees and categories | Capitalizes words as you type |
| Remove memorized payees not used in last (months, 0 = never) | Clears out old payees |

### Notifications

Each asks before saving a transaction that may be a mistake:

| Setting | Asks when |
|:---|:---|
| When entering out-of-date transactions | The date is over 7 days past or 30 days ahead |
| A check number is reused | The number is already used in the account |
| When entering uncategorized transactions | A line, split lines included, has no category |
| Ask before saving changes to an existing transaction | You change a transaction already saved |

### Backups

See [Backups and Restore](backups.md).

## Themes and Fonts

Three lists at the right end of the menu bar:

- **Theme**: Nordic, Light, Dark, Classic, or Matrix.
- **Font**: System, Arial, Verdana, or Courier New.
- **Font size**: 10 to 24; everything grows with it.

They take effect at once, and are kept for this computer rather than
in the book.

::: tip
Negative amounts always carry a minus sign as well as a color, so
they read the same in every theme, whatever colors you can tell
apart.
:::

## The Navigation Bar

The bar under the menus starts with **Back** and **Forward**, which
step through the screens you've visited, then buttons for the
screens you use most: Insights, Reminders, Calendar, Reconcile, and
Investments to start with. The search box is at its end.

Choose **Edit > Navigation Bar…** to change the buttons:

- **Available** lists everything that can go on the bar: any menu
  item, Investments, and any account.
- **On the bar (left to right)** lists what's there now.
- **Add ›** and **‹ Remove** move an item between the two; **▲ Up**
  and **▼ Down** change the order.
- **Reset to default** puts back the starting set.

Click **Save**.

::: tip
Put your checking account and the reports you run most on the bar.
They're then one click away from anywhere.
:::
