# Budgets

::: {.note title="At a glance"}
A **budget** sets an amount for each category and month, then measures
what you actually spent and received against it. **Graph View** shows
one period as bars; **Annual View** shows the whole year month by
month. A book can hold several budgets.
:::

## Create a Budget

Choose **Budget > Create Budget…** and give it a name.

Kansha starts you off. It picks the categories you used in the last
12 months, and sets each month to that category's average over the 12
whole months before this one, in whole dollars. Change any of it
afterward.

Open a budget again from the **Budget** menu, where each one is listed
by name.

## How Actuals Count

| Item | How it counts |
|:---|:---|
| Spending | Shown positive; a refund lowers it |
| Income | Shown positive |
| A split | Each line counts in its own category |
| Accounts | All of them, investment accounts included |
| Income and Investment Fees inside an IRA, 401(k), Roth, HSA, or DAF | Not counted; that money isn't yours to spend yet |
| Other spending from those accounts | Counted |
| Money leaving those accounts for your own accounts | Counted as income, in **Retirement Distributions** |
| Other transfers, Opening Balance | Never counted |
| A subcategory not in the budget | Counts in its budgeted parent |
| Any other category | Goes in **Everything Else**; click ▶ to list them |

**Balance** is Budget less Actual, for income as for expenses. Every
transaction dated in a month counts, even a later-dated one.

The band across the top covers expenses only: what you've spent, a bar
against the budget, and the amount **LEFT** or **OVER**. Being over
budget is said in words with ⚠, and the bar is striped.

## Graph View

Set **View** to **Graph View**, then choose a **Period**: Monthly,
Quarterly, Yearly, Quarter to date, Year to date, Last 3 months, or
Last 6 months. Monthly, Quarterly, and Yearly add ◀ ▶ to move between
periods, starting on the current one. Last 3 and Last 6 months are
full months, before the current one.

Each row's bar is as wide as its budget, with the actual written in
it. It fills as you spend. Over budget, the whole bar is striped, and
the part past the budget is lighter. To the right is **N left**,
**N over**, or **N under** (for a negative budget), then the period's
**Budget**.

**Change a period's budget:** point at a row's Budget and type. The
amount is spread over the period's months, with any remainder in the
last month. A whole-dollar amount spreads in whole dollars.

## Annual View

Set **View** to **Annual View**. Use ◀ ▶ to change the year, and
**Show** to choose **Details**, **Budget**, **Balance**, or **Actual**.
**Details** shows all three for past and current months, and Budget
alone for future ones. The current month is shaded, with a **To Date**
column after it, this year only; the last column sums up the year.

**Edit a budget:** click a Budget cell and type. Enter moves down, and
Tab moves right.

**Copy amounts** (with Details or Budget showing):

| Click | To |
|:---|:---|
| ▾ on a month's Budget heading | Copy that month, every category, to future, past, or all months |
| ⇉ beside a category | Copy one month across that row |
| ▾ in a Budget cell (or Alt+Down) | Apply forward, apply to the whole year, or set the month from the category's average |

**See the transactions:** click any Actual figure, in Annual View or
Graph View.

## Another Year

Moving to a year the budget doesn't hold asks whether to create it:
copy its budgets, or only its categories, from the nearest year held.
Last 3 and Last 6 months don't ask; months outside the budget count
as 0. A year not held shows budgets of 0 and says so.

## The Gear

| Item | What it does |
|:---|:---|
| Select categories to budget… | Choose the income and expense categories, at any level |
| Copy this budget… | A new budget with the same categories and amounts |
| Edit budget name… | Rename it |
| Delete this budget… | Removes the budget; your transactions aren't touched |
| Create new budget… | Starts another |

Budget reports are planned for a later version.

## Good to Know

- Deleting a category removes its budget amounts. Merging one adds
  them into the category it's merged with.
- Balance Adjustment, from reconciling, counts only if you budget it.
