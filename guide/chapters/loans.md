# Loans

::: {.note title="At a glance"}
Give a Loan account its terms, and Kansha works out each payment's
split between principal and interest. It also adds the payment
reminder for you. Every split comes from the register's balance, so
an extra or late payment shows in the next one.
:::

## Set Up a Loan

Make the account first: see [Accounts](accounts.md). Saving a new
Loan account opens the loan screen. To open it later, click **Loan
details…** on the loan's register, or use the register's gear menu.

**Loan details:**

| Field | What to enter |
|:---|:---|
| Type | Mortgage, Auto, Consumer, Student, Commercial, Business, Construction, or Home Equity |
| Opening date | The day the loan began |
| Original balance | What you borrowed |
| Interest rate (%) | The annual rate |
| Length | In years or months |
| Compounding | Daily, monthly, or semi-annually; your loan papers say which |
| Payment schedule | How often you pay, from twice a year to weekly |
| Current balance | What you owe before the next payment |

The **Payment** shows as soon as the terms are in, principal and
interest only. If your lender's figure differs, tick **Use the
lender's payment figure** and type it.

**Payment:** the next due date, the lender, the account payments come
from, and the category for interest. Then add any **other lines**,
such as escrow or insurance, each with a category and an amount, and
any **extra principal** you pay each time. The screen shows the next
payment's principal and interest and the total.

Click **Save**.

::: {.note title="The current balance"}
If the balance you typed differs from the register's, Kansha asks,
then adds one transaction to Opening Balance for the difference. Your
past entries stay as they are, and no expense or income is recorded.
:::

## The Payment Reminder

Saving adds a reminder for the payment, shown 7 days before it's due
in **Reminders** and on the **Calendar**. Enter it as any other
reminder: see [Scheduled Transactions](scheduled.md).

The amounts are worked out each time, so don't expect them to match
last month's. To change the reminder, change the loan: editing it
opens the loan screen. It can't be deleted by itself; it goes when
the loan's account does.

The **# left** count follows the balance, so paying extra shortens it.
The last payment pays off what's left, and the reminder ends.

## When the Rate Changes

Type the new rate on the loan screen, and Kansha asks for the date it
takes effect. Each payment uses the rate in effect on its due date.
Older rates stay in the loan's history. A payment Kansha worked out is
worked out again from the new rate; a figure you typed stays until you
change it.

## See the Amortization Table

The loan screen shows, on request, every payment from the current
balance on: its date, amount, principal, interest, extra principal,
and the balance after it. It also gives the payoff date and total
interest, with and without your extra principal.

## Good to Know

- Interest comes from the balance the day before the payment, so a
  transaction you enter by hand in the loan's register changes the
  next split.
- If interest is ever more than the payment, the payment is interest
  alone, and the loan screen warns you.
- A closed loan account keeps its terms. An account with transactions
  can't be deleted, only closed: see [Accounts](accounts.md).
