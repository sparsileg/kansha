# Recipes

::: {.note title="At a glance"}
Short step-by-step answers to everyday jobs. Each points to the
chapter with the details.
:::

## Pay the Credit Card

The payment is a transfer from checking to the card.

1. Open the checking register.
2. Enter the date, the payee (the card company), and the amount under
   **Payment**.
3. In **Category**, choose the credit card account.

The payment shows in both registers. To pay it every month, right-click
it and choose **Schedule this…** (see [Scheduled
Transactions](scheduled.md)).

## Enter a Paycheck

1. Open the checking register. Enter the date, the employer as payee,
   and the net pay that reached the bank under **Deposit**.
2. Click **Split**.
3. Add a line for the gross salary in **Salary**, as a positive
   amount.
4. Add a line for each deduction, with a leading `-`: `-412.50` in
   **Tax:Fed**, `-118.20` in **Tax:State**, `-250.00` to the 401(k)
   account as a transfer, and so on.
5. When **Remainder** shows **✓**, click **Enter**.

Then right-click it and choose **Schedule this…**, with **Twice a
month** or **Weekly** every 2 weeks. Each payday, enter it from Due
and overdue and change any amounts that changed.

## Take Cash from an ATM

A transfer from checking to your Cash account: in checking, enter the
amount under **Payment**, with **Cash** as the category. Spend from
the Cash account as you like. If you don't track cash that closely,
use an expense category of your own for it instead of a transfer.

## Record a Refund

In the account the refund went to, enter it with the same payee and
category as the purchase, with the amount in the other column:
**Deposit** for a bank, **Payment** for a credit card. The category's
total goes down, as it should.

## Move Money to Savings

In checking, enter the amount under **Payment**, with the savings
account as the category. To save every month, schedule it.

## Split a Store Receipt

A purchase that was part groceries and part household goods:

1. Enter the total as usual, then click **Split**.
2. One line: the grocery part in **Food:Groceries**.
3. Second line: the rest in **Household**. **Remainder** reaches ✓.

## Fix a Category on Many Transactions

1. **Edit > Find/Replace…**.
2. Find: **Category**, **Exactly**, the wrong category. Add a row for
   the **Payee** if only some of them are wrong.
3. **Select all**, or tick the ones to change.
4. Replace **Category** with the right one and click **Replace all**.

One **Undo** reverses it (see [The Register](register.md)).

## Clean Up Duplicate Payees

**Tools > Memorized Payees**, click the misspelled one, and **Merge**
it into the right one. Every transaction moves across.

## Give Shares to Charity

In the investment account, enter **Shares removed**, choose the
security and shares, enter the day's price, and name the charity
under **Given to**. The Tax Schedule lists the gift at its value (see
[Investments](investments.md)).

## Record a Required Distribution from an IRA

In the IRA register, **Sell** what the plan sold, if anything, then
**Cash out** the distribution to checking. Enter any tax held back
with **Tax withheld**.

## Track a Trip or Project

Make a tag, such as `Maine 2026`, in **Tools > Tags**. Tag each
transaction for it as you enter it, split lines included. Then run
**Reports > Spending > Itemized Categories** and choose just that tag
under **Customize… > Tags** (see [Reports](reports.md)).

## See What You Spent Last Year

**Reports > Spending > Income/Expense by Category**, **Date range**
**Last year**. Set **Interval** to **Month** to see each month side
by side. Click any amount for the transactions.

## Get Ready for Taxes

In January or February, after the year's statements are reconciled:

1. **Reports > Tax > Tax Schedule**, **Last year**: each tax line with
   its transactions. **Save PDF…** for your records or your preparer.
2. **Reports > Tax > Capital Gains**, **Last year**: compare it with
   each broker's 1099-B.
3. **Reports > Investing > Investment Income**, **Last year**: compare
   it with the 1099-DIV and 1099-INT forms.
4. Look at **Uncategorized** in Needs attention; a missing category
   may be a missing deduction.

## Use Kansha on Two Computers

Kansha is for one person at a time. To use it on two computers, keep
the book in one place and carry it: on a USB drive, or by restoring
the newest backup on the other computer each time. Never open the
same book on two computers at once, or from a folder that syncs both
ways while it's open.

::: why
A synced folder copies the book file while Kansha is writing it. Two
copies changed at once can't be merged, and one set of changes is
lost.
:::
