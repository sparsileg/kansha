# Glossary {.appendix}

**Account.** Anywhere money is kept or owed. See
[Accounts](accounts.md).

**Asset.** Something you own: money in the bank, investments, a
house. It adds to net worth.

**Asset class.** The kind of investment a security is, such as U.S.
stocks, international stocks, bonds, or cash, for the Asset
Allocation report.

**Backup.** One encrypted file holding the whole book, made by itself
or by **File > Back Up Now**. See [Backups and Restore](backups.md).

**Backup passphrase.** The passphrase that opens the book and its
backups. Lost, it can't be recovered.

**Book.** Everything Kansha keeps for one household: two files,
`<name>.db` and `<name>.key`. Quicken calls it a data file.

**Built-in category.** A category Kansha itself needs, such as
Opening Balance or Uncategorized. Only its tax settings and Hidden
can change.

**Category.** What money was for or from: Groceries, Salary. Income
or Expense, and nested with `:`.

**Cleared.** Seen on the bank's side; `c` in the register.

**Cost basis.** What shares cost, commissions included. The gain on a
sale is the proceeds minus the cost basis.

**Entry line.** The empty line at the bottom of a register where new
transactions are typed.

**Estimate.** A scheduled amount worked out as the average of past
payments.

**Holding.** The shares of one security in one account.

**Inbox.** The folder beside the book, `<book>-inbox`, where entries
from the command line wait for Kansha to read them.

**Insight.** A tab of cards on the home page. See
[Insights](insights.md).

**Liability.** Something you owe: a credit card, a loan. It takes
away from net worth.

**Lot.** One purchase of a security: its date, shares, and cost.

**Lot method.** The rule that picks which lots a sale takes.

**Memorized payee.** A payee Kansha remembers, with the category,
memo, and amount of its last use, to fill them in next time.

**Navigation bar.** The row of buttons under the menus.

**Needs attention.** The Insights card that lists problems in the
book.

**Net worth.** Assets minus liabilities.

**Opening Balance.** The built-in category of each account's first
transaction, its starting balance.

**Payee.** Who money went to or came from.

**Reconcile.** Check an account against its statement; the matched
transactions become `R`. See [Reconciling](reconciling.md).

**Register.** An account's list of transactions.

**Reminder.** A schedule in **Remind me** mode, or the Reminders
view that lists all schedules.

**Schedule.** A transaction that repeats. See [Scheduled
Transactions](scheduled.md).

**Security.** A stock, fund, bond, or anything else with shares and a
price.

**Split.** One transaction with several categories or transfers.

**Stale price.** A price older than the days set in Settings
(7 to start with).

**Tag.** A label that marks transactions across categories; Quicken
calls it a class.

**Tax line.** The line of a tax form a category or transfer belongs
on.

**Tax treatment.** How the IRS sees an account: taxable,
tax-deferred, or tax-exempt.

**Transfer.** Money moved between two of your accounts, entered once
and shown in both.

**True up.** Make Kansha's lots match the broker's, from the broker's
cost-basis file.

**Unverified.** An entry from the command line or a file, waiting for
review. It changes nothing until approved.

**Void.** Keep a transaction but make its amount zero, as for a
cancelled check.
